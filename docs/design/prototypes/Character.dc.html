Crumb, the ratewatch cookie

Crumb's mood follows rate-limit status, so a user can tell where they stand at a glance. Crumb is the brand mark in the app bar and the button on the clicker page.

OK Under 90% used
Face
Five stages, one per 20 requests (below); starts with closed happy eyes
Idle
Slow bob, 8 px, 3 s loop, ease in-out
Says
Plenty left, keep clicking
Warning 90% or more used
Face
Wide eyes, worried brows, wobbly mouth, sweat drop, one chip left
Idle
Nervous jitter, ±3°, in short bursts every 4 s
Says
Ten or fewer left, slow down
Exceeded 100% used
Face
Bite taken out, no chips, crossed-out eyes, open mouth, crumbs
Idle
Slow slump and tilt, 4 s loop
Says
Out of requests, wait for the countdown
Wait
A teal ring fills while the bite grows back; eyes rest at halfway; a hop and “Welcome back” on reset

Progress stages within OK

Crumb drops one chocolate chip every 20 requests, so his chips double as a rough gauge of what is left. A short note explains each drop.
Fresh0–19 used · 5 chipsClosed happy eyes, soft smile
Warming up20–39 used · 4 chipsEyes open, bigger grin, rosier cheeks
Excited40–59 used · 3 chipsSquinting joy, open-mouth laugh
Focused60–79 used · 2 chipsDetermined brows, confident smirk
Tired80–89 used · 1 chipHeavy lids, slower bob; Warning comes next

Strokes thicken below 48 px so the face still reads.

Press

Squash to 90 × 84%, overshoot, settle: 250 ms. A “−1” floats up. The request fires on click, not when the animation ends.

Reduced motion

  • No idle loop, squish or floating “−1”
  • Mood still changes: the face carries the status
  • Requests left is still announced to screen readers
  • Ring and bite update in steps, no hop on reset
  • Clicking works exactly the same
